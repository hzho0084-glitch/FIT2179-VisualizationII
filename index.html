Australian Sports

This visualisation explores how Australians connect with sport through participation, location, gender, age, attendance and venue use. The story focuses on how different sports build different pathways into Australian sporting culture.

Key message: Australian sport is not dominated by one single pattern. Different sports attract people through different entry points, regions and audiences.

1. How do people enter different sports?

This stacked bar chart compares the main participation pathways across sports. School sport and friends are important entry points, but media exposure and family influence also shape how people first connect with sport.

2. Which sports dominate different Australian states?

This map-style tile visualisation gives a quick geographic overview of the leading sport in each state or territory. It works as a simplified map for the peer review version and can later be upgraded into a full choropleth map.

3. How does age affect sport preference?

This heatmap shows how strongly each age group is connected with each sport. Darker cells indicate stronger interest or participation.

4. How has attendance changed over time?

This streamgraph-style area chart compares how attendance patterns change across sports over several years.

5. How do funding and participation compare?

This connected scatter plot compares funding and participation. It helps reveal which sports have high participation but relatively lower funding.

6. Which sports convert fans into active participants?

This funnel chart shows how the audience narrows from awareness to active participation.

7. Which venues are used most effectively?

This lollipop chart compares venue capacity and actual usage. It highlights the gap between available sporting infrastructure and real use.

8. Australian Sports Geography

This choropleth map shows the dominant sport associated with each Australian state or territory. The colour represents the leading sport, while the tooltip shows the state and sport name.

Conclusion

Overall, Australian sport is shaped by a combination of place, community, school, gender, media and infrastructure. The most successful sports are not only popular; they also create strong pathways from casual interest to long-term participation.
